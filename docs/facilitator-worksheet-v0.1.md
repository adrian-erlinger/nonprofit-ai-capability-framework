Proportionate AI-fit checklist:

- [ ] The task is repeated and information-intensive.
- [ ] Inputs or source materials are sufficiently reliable.
- [ ] A human can review, correct, or override outputs.
- [ ] Success can be measured.
- [ ] Risk is proportionate to available controls.
- [ ] AI adds value beyond existing software or traditional automation.
- [ ] The organization could maintain the workflow after a test.

These checks do not produce a score or automatic decision. Their importance depends on the workflow's context, consequence, and available controls.
