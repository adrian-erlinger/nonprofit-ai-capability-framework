## Organizational Concern

"Members keep asking us questions that we know we have answered somewhere, but neither they nor our staff can reliably find the current answer."

This matters now because repeated questions consume staff capacity, members receive inconsistent answers, and leadership is asking whether AI could improve member service.

## Desired Outcome

Members and staff can find current, authoritative answers quickly and consistently while reducing repeated staff effort.

## Selected Workflow

- Workflow boundary: When a member or staff person asks a recurring information question, membership and program staff use the website, member portal, SharePoint, shared drives, email, PDFs, and staff knowledge to produce an answer or escalation for the member or requesting staff person.
- Trigger: A member or staff person asks a recurring question about association resources, benefits, programs, webinars, research, or member information.
- Roles: Membership staff, program staff, communications staff, technology support, and content owners.
- Inputs and systems: Member questions, webpages, PDFs, portal content, SharePoint files, shared-drive documents, emails, staff knowledge, WordPress, Microsoft 365, SharePoint, shared drives, and email.
- Output: Current, authoritative answer or escalation.
- Recipient: Member or staff requester.
- Human review or escalation point: Staff escalate when the answer seems outdated, restricted to members, unclear, or owned by another department.

## Owner and Stakeholders

- Accountable owner: Jordan Lee.
- Staff performing the work: Membership and program staff.
- Reviewer or approver: Jordan Lee, with content owners for specific answers.
- Affected members or constituencies: NACPM members seeking information about resources, benefits, programs, webinars, research, and services.
- Supporting functions: Membership, programs, communications, and technology staff.

## Current-State Diagnosis

### Observable Friction

- Members and staff cannot reliably find current answers.
- Members sometimes receive different answers depending on which staff member responds.
- Staff search across multiple systems and sometimes copy from outdated PDFs.
- Similar information exists in multiple formats and locations.

### Supporting Evidence

- Membership and program staff receive approximately 120 repeated information requests per month.
- Staff estimate that responding, searching, verifying, and routing these questions consumes approximately six staff hours per week.
- Several important resources do not have a named content owner.
- Current website and portal search functions are inconsistent.
- Some resources are public, while others are restricted to members.

### Current Workarounds

- Staff search the website, portal, SharePoint, shared drives, email, and old PDFs.
- Staff ask colleagues where current answers live.
- Staff reuse prior responses or older documents when pressed for time.
- Some staff use public AI tools inconsistently for drafting without shared governance.

### Root-Cause Hypotheses

- Hypothesis: NACPM lacks a reliable inventory of common member questions and authoritative answers.
- Hypothesis: Content ownership is unclear across departments.
- Hypothesis: Duplicate, outdated, or poorly organized content causes inconsistent responses.
- Hypothesis: Staff are compensating for a content-governance problem with individual memory and manual search.

### Evidence Still Needed

- Top 25 repeated member questions.
- Authoritative source and owner for each question.
- Outdated, duplicated, missing, and restricted content.
- Baseline answer accuracy.
- Baseline staff response time.
- Whether existing search or navigation improvements resolve a meaningful share of the problem.
- Member and staff feedback on clarity and consistency.

## Interventions Considered

### Non-AI Interventions

- Content inventory.
- Assignment of content owners.
- Retirement or consolidation of outdated and duplicated content.
- A governed FAQ for the most common questions.
- Improved information architecture.

### Existing-System or Traditional Automation Options

- Website and portal search configuration.
- Structured routing or escalation.
- Deterministic links or guided navigation.
- Automated content-review reminders.

### Possible AI-Supported Intervention

- A later governed retrieval assistant using approved sources, citations, staff review, member-access controls, and a human escalation path.

## AI-Suitability Conclusion and Rationale

- Selected conclusion: Prepare first, then reconsider AI.
- Rationale: AI may eventually add value because the task is repeated and information-intensive. NACPM does not yet have authoritative content, content ownership, access rules, or baseline evidence needed to responsibly test an AI-supported retrieval workflow. Existing search, portal configuration, content governance, or traditional automation may resolve a meaningful share of the problem.
- Conditions that could change the conclusion: The top 25 questions are identified; authoritative sources and owners are assigned; outdated, duplicated, missing, and restricted content is flagged; baseline accuracy and response time are established; existing search or navigation improvements are tested; member-access rules and staff review practices are defined.

## Material Risks and Constraints

- Source authority and accuracy: Authoritative sources and content owners are not yet mapped; no accuracy baseline exists; outdated PDFs are sometimes used.
- Access, privacy, and security: Some resources are member-restricted, so access rules must be clarified before any AI retrieval test.
- Trust and member experience: Members may lose trust if answers conflict, cite outdated content, or expose restricted information.
- Human review and staff capability: Staff review and escalation would be required; staff currently use AI inconsistently and lack shared practices.
- Existing technology and maintenance: Website and portal search may be improved before AI is tested; content ownership and review cycles are prerequisites for maintaining any future retrieval workflow.
